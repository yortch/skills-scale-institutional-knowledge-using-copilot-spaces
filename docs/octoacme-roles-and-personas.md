# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Facilitates agile ceremonies and helps remove blockers to enable team productivity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Help identify and remove blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Shield team from external interruptions and distractions
- Track and communicate team velocity and capacity

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement
- Ensure agile practices are followed consistently

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Weekly sync with PM on blockers and team health
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Interactions
Works closely with PM, PdM, Developers, and QA to drive continuous improvement and remove impediments.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Coordinates deployments, release communications, and ensures production readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases with stakeholders
- Coordinate deployment steps across teams
- Communicate release timelines and status updates
- Prepare and validate rollback plans
- Manage release documentation and change logs
- Coordinate go/no-go decisions for releases

### Goals
- Ensure safe, predictable releases to production
- Minimize deployment-related incidents
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings
- Deployment status updates
- Post-release reports and metrics

### Interactions
Collaborates with PM and Developers, ensures QA sign-off, coordinates with Support/Operations Lead, updates stakeholders.

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
Designs and validates user experiences, prototypes, and interface specifications.

### Responsibilities
- Design user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Conduct usability testing and gather user feedback
- Create design systems and maintain consistency
- Provide detailed specs for acceptance criteria
- Validate implementations match design intent

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Ensure design consistency across products
- Validate solutions through user research and testing

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critiques
- User research findings presentations
- Acceptance criteria refinement sessions

### Interactions
Works with PdM for requirements definition and prioritization, collaborates with Developers during implementation, participates in acceptance testing.

---

## Support/Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Ensures production stability, monitors systems, and coordinates incident response.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor production systems and error rates
- Triage and coordinate response to incidents
- Ensure operational readiness for releases
- Manage on-call schedules and escalations
- Track and communicate SLA/SLO metrics
- Provide feedback on operational pain points

### Goals
- Maintain high system availability and reliability
- Minimize mean time to resolution (MTTR) for incidents
- Ensure smooth production operations

### Typical Communication
- Incident reports and postmortems
- Weekly operational metrics reviews
- Release readiness sign-offs

### Interactions
Communicates with PM for prioritization, coordinates with Release Manager for deployments, works with Developers on incident resolution and operational improvements.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Bridges business and technical teams by gathering, analyzing, and refining requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Analyze current processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Write detailed user stories and specifications
- Validate solutions meet business needs
- Facilitate requirements workshops with stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure clear, complete requirements that meet business objectives
- Reduce rework from unclear or incomplete specifications
- Improve alignment between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions
- User story refinement
- Acceptance criteria validation

### Interactions
Liaisons between business stakeholders, PdM, and Developers to ensure requirements are understood and properly implemented.

---

## QA/Testing Engineer

### Role Summary
Validates quality and ensures acceptance criteria are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans and test cases
- Perform manual and automated testing
- Validate acceptance criteria are met
- Report and track defects
- Participate in release go/no-go decisions
- Maintain test automation frameworks

### Goals
- Ensure high quality releases with minimal defects
- Reduce regression issues through comprehensive testing
- Improve test coverage and automation

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews
- Defect triage meetings
- QA sign-off for releases

### Interactions
Works with Developers on defect resolution, coordinates with Release Manager for release readiness, validates designs with UX/UI Designer.
